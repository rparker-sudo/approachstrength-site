How it works

  1. 1

    Build the season.

    Pick positions, season phase, and match days. Approach Strength builds the program from your choices, and you can change any of it. Every athlete gets their level's version of each exercise.

  2. 2

    Athletes log on their phones.

    Today's workout, sets, reps, and rest timers, at school or at home.

  3. 3

    Run the room from the board.

    Put the live board on a screen: who's lifting, who's resting, who's done. Never weights.

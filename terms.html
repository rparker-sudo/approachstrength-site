Terms (Soft Launch)

Last updated: November 16, 2026

  1. Test version. Approach Strength is free and in an early test, run by Ryan Parker ("we"). It may change, have bugs, or stop.
  2. Eligibility. 13+. Under 18 requires a signed parent/guardian consent form on file with the coach of each team. Coaches are 18+ and must accept §4 before recording consent or running testing days. Personal trainer teams are for adults only (18+). Trainers must accept §4T and be confirmed by us before clients can join.
  3. Training risk. Strength and conditioning training, including jump tests and strength checks, can cause injury. Approach Strength gives general training information, not medical advice. Learn new lifts with a qualified coach, use weights you can control, never grind out a rep, and stop if something hurts. You (or your parent, for minors) accept these risks.
  4. Coach Terms. If you use Approach Strength as a coach, you agree to the following. You accept them with a separate checkbox when you create or next use a coach account, and we record the version and date.
    1. You run your training. Approach Strength is a tool. You decide what your athletes do, and you're responsible for the programs you post or edit, how they're carried out, and supervising your athletes, as your school, club, or role requires. Programs Approach Strength generates follow built-in limits (levels, effort, rest, weekly caps). Anything you build or edit is yours: the app doesn't limit it, and you're fully responsible for it, including turning off level adjustment. Testing-day rules (supervision, eligibility) apply to everyone.
    2. Permission. You're allowed to coach the athletes you invite. Before any athlete under 18 logs anything, you have written approval to use Approach Strength with your team from your school (athletic director or principal) or club director, and you send us a copy at ryan@approachstrength.com. You follow your school's or club's rules, including any app-approval, phone, and screen rules.
    3. Parent consent. You give each parent the current Approach Strength consent form, record exactly the boxes they checked, keep the signed form, and email us a copy. You never record consent you don't have. You remove anyone you learn is under 13 and tell us.
    4. Athlete readiness. You only include athletes cleared to train under your school's or club's rules (for example, a current sports physical where required). You set and confirm each athlete's experience level honestly. Strength checks for minors unlock only at a level you've confirmed as intermediate or advanced.
    5. Testing days. You personally supervise every testing day, with safety bars or spotters set, before confirming supervision in the app. You stop any set when form breaks, and you never run a true one-rep max with minors.
    6. Safety and emergencies. You follow your school's or club's emergency, injury, and concussion procedures. Approach Strength isn't an emergency or medical service. If an athlete is hurt during a session run with Approach Strength, you tell us at ryan@approachstrength.com within 72 hours (no medical details needed: date, team, what session, and that it was reported to the school or club).
    7. Athlete data. You use athletes' data only to coach them. You don't copy, export, screenshot, post, or share it outside your team and its parents, and you don't use it for recruiting, discipline, or anything unrelated to training. You keep your login private and sign out on shared devices.
    8. Screens. You don't show the coach board on school- or district-owned screens or networks unless your school has approved it, and you make sure only your team can see it.
    9. Your duties stay yours. Nothing in these Terms changes your duties to your athletes, your school or club, or the law (including any mandated-reporter duties). We don't monitor athletes and can't do those things for you.

If your employer or club doesn't allow you to accept this paragraph, tell us before using Approach Strength with athletes.
    4T. Trainer Terms. If you use Approach Strength as a personal trainer with a client team ("Training clients, adults 18+"), these terms apply instead of §4 and §4A. You accept them with separate checkboxes when you create a client team, and we record the version and date. If we change them, you'll need to accept again before posting new programs.

    1. You're an independent professional. You train clients through your own business. You aren't our employee, agent, or partner, and we aren't a party to your agreements with your clients or your gym. Approach Strength is software only. It doesn't provide training, supervise anyone, or check your work.
    2. Confirmations. Before your first client can join, you confirm to us in writing, by email to ryan@approachstrength.com: (a) your current certification (name and issuing body); (b) that you carry your own professional liability insurance (insurer and expiry date); and (c) that you train clients through your own business. You keep these current while you use Approach Strength with clients, and you tell us right away if any of them lapses.     3. You run your training. You decide what your clients do, and you're responsible for the programs you give them, how they're carried out, and any supervision. Screening clients before they train (health questions, medical clearance, and deciding whether a program suits them) is your job, done outside the app. Approach Strength doesn't screen clients. Programs Approach Strength generates follow built-in limits. Anything you build or edit is yours: the app doesn't limit it, and you're fully responsible for it.
    4. Adults only, no medical services. Only clients 18 or older. Don't use Approach Strength for injury rehab, post-surgery or medical programs, pregnancy programs, nutrition or diet plans, or weight-loss programs. "Back after a break" is for clients returning after time off. Clients returning from an injury or medical condition should be cleared by a qualified professional first.
    5. Client data. You use clients' data only to train that client. You don't copy, export, screenshot, post, or share it, including with your gym, other trainers, or for marketing. You don't require clients to share their check-ins as a condition of training with you, and you respect their choices in Settings. You don't ask clients to put health details in the app. You keep your login private, and you don't let anyone else (including gym staff) use it.
    6. Your gym and our name. You don't put the Approach Strength name on anything your gym prints, posts, or shares (flyers, signs, social posts, class schedules, websites, or member emails), and you don't present it as your gym's service. You tell clients about it one to one. You follow your gym's rules on phones and screens, and you show the team board only to your own session.
    7. Safety and incidents. You follow your gym's and your profession's emergency and injury procedures. Approach Strength isn't an emergency or medical service. If a client is hurt during a session you ran with Approach Strength, you tell us at ryan@approachstrength.com within 72 hours (date, team, what session; no medical details).

What Approach Strength is

Approach Strength is a free, early-version web app for volleyball strength training. The coach posts workouts, and your athlete logs them on a phone. It is in a small test ("soft launch") before a public release. There is nothing to buy.

This form covers only the team and coach named above. If your athlete is also on another team that uses Approach Strength (for example, a school team and a club team), that team's coach needs a separate signed form, and that coach sees only what your athlete does with that team.

Risks of physical training

Strength, power, and conditioning training, including jump tests and strength checks, carries a risk of injury, from muscle strains and sprains to more serious injury. Strength checks involve heavy lifting. In Approach Strength they are only for intermediate and advanced athletes on a coached team, only after the coach confirms in the app that they are supervising with safety bars or spotters, never as a true one-rep max, and never on a day the athlete reports pain or being run down. Approach Strength gives general training information. It is not medical advice and doesn't replace supervision by a qualified coach. Athletes should stop if something hurts and tell a coach, parent, or athletic trainer about any pain or injury in person.

Your choices

Check each box you agree to. Box 1 is required. Boxes 2–6 are optional; the app works without them, and each matching feature stays off for your athlete.

1. Participation (required). I am the parent or legal guardian of the athlete named above. I agree that they may use Approach Strength under the Approach Strength Terms and Privacy Notice. I understand the risks of physical training described above and accept them for my athlete.

2. Daily check-ins. My athlete may log the daily check-in (sleep, soreness, energy, pain yes/no).

3. Sharing check-ins with the coach (requires box 2). The coach named above may see my athlete's check-ins.

4. Team boards. My athlete's name ("Jordan M." or initials) and workout progress may appear on the coach's live board (including which rack group they're in), the team effort leaderboard, and teammates' "your rack" card in the app. Teammates can see all of these.

5. Attendance and hours. My athlete's practice and match attendance and hours trained may be recorded and shown on the effort leaderboard.

6. Testing days. My athlete may take part in jump tests and, if eligible, coach-supervised strength checks, and the results may be saved and used to set their training weights.

Changing your mind

You can withdraw any choice or ask us to delete your athlete's account and data at any time. Tell the coach, or email ryan@approachstrength.com. Your athlete can also delete their account in the app (Settings → Delete my account). Deleted data is removed within 30 days. We keep a short record of what you agreed to, and of coach supervision on testing days, for 5 years after the account closes, as proof. That record contains no workout, check-in, or test results.
